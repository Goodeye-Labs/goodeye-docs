# Notifications

Goodeye emails you about the changes that affect you: access you gained or lost,
ownership handoffs you are part of, and account or billing events. There are two
groups. Collaboration notifications keep you in the loop as you share and receive
work, and you can turn them off. Account notifications carry information you need
about your own account and billing, so they are always sent.

## Collaboration notifications

You receive a collaboration email when:

- A workflow is shared with you, directly or through a team you belong to, so
  you can start running it right away.
- You are invited to a team. The email links to the invitation so you can accept
  it before it expires.
- Someone proposes transferring a workflow, template, or team to you. You accept
  the transfer from the email.
- An ownership transfer you started is accepted. You proposed handing a workflow,
  template, or team to someone else, they accepted, and you no longer own it.
- You are removed from a team. You lose the access that team gave you.

Every collaboration email includes an unsubscribe link. Using it turns off all
collaboration notifications for your account, and you can turn them back on from
the same page at any time. A per-recipient daily limit also caps how many of
these emails you can receive in a rolling 24 hours, so a burst of activity cannot
flood your inbox.

## Account notifications

You receive an account email when:

- Your account is suspended. The email explains how to reach support.
- Your monthly balance is used up. The email tells you when your credits reset.
  It is sent at most once per billing period, not on every blocked request.

Account notifications are always delivered. They are not covered by the
collaboration unsubscribe link, and the per-recipient daily limit does not apply
to them, because they carry information you need about your account and billing.
They are low volume by design.

## Turning notifications on or off

The unsubscribe link in any collaboration email opens a page where you can turn
collaboration email off or back on for your account. Account notifications are
not affected by this preference.
